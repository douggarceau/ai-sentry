Email Header Analyzer

Paste an email's full headers to see whether it really came from who it claims, using the same SPF, DKIM and DMARC checks your mail server ran.

Runs in your browser. Nothing you enter is sent anywhere.

How to copy the headers

  • Gmail (web): open the email, click the three dots next to Reply, choose Show original, then Copy to clipboard.
  • Outlook (web or new Outlook): open the email, click the three dots, choose View, then View message source.
  • Apple Mail (Mac): open the email, then choose View, Message, All Headers.
  • Yahoo Mail: open the email, click the three dots, choose View raw message.
