How to share less

  • Use a browser with fingerprinting protection: Firefox (Enhanced Tracking Protection set to Strict), Brave, Safari, or Tor Browser for the strongest protection.
  • Turn on Global Privacy Control. It tells sites not to sell or share your data, and some state privacy laws, including California's, require sites to honor it.
  • Install fewer unusual fonts and extensions: every rare one makes you easier to single out.
  • A VPN hides your IP address, but not anything on this page.
