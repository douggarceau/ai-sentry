Photo Location Remover

Phone photos often hide the exact GPS location where they were taken, plus the camera and time. See what yours reveals and save a clean copy.

Runs in your browser. Nothing you enter is sent anywhere.

The photo is read on your device and never uploaded.

Stop new photos recording your location

  • iPhone: Settings › Privacy & Security › Location Services › Camera › Never. When sharing a photo, tap Options at the top of the share sheet and turn off Location.
  • Android: open the Camera app's settings and turn off Location tags (or Save location). Google Photos can also remove location when you share.
