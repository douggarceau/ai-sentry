What it finds

The AI attack surface, inventoried.

AI tools now read files, browse the web and act on their own. AI Sentry shows where that access lives and where it can be turned against you.

AI apps and assistants

Every installed AI app and chat assistant, with the files, microphone, screen and accounts each one is allowed to use.

Autonomous agents

AI agents and their tool connections, flagged when they can write files, send messages or run commands without a person approving.

Browser extensions

AI-powered extensions that can read every page you open, including email, banking and internal systems.

Exposed AI keys

Keys for AI services left in plain files, scripts and settings, where anyone or anything on the machine can take them.

Prompt-injection traps

Hidden text in documents, web pages and files that is written to hijack an AI assistant when it reads them.

Shadow AI and local models

Unapproved AI tools and locally running models, and whether any of them is listening on the network.

How it works

One scan. A clear answer.

Threat model

How an AI attack actually happens.

The attacker never touches your computer. They plant instructions where your AI will read them, and let your AI do the work with the access you already gave it.

Indirect prompt injection · kill chainMITRE ATLAS AML.T0051
Detection engine

Under the hood.

A local, rules-driven pipeline. Collectors read the system state, a normalizer turns it into one graph of AI components and their privileges, and the rule engine scores every edge in that graph.

Security and privacy

Built to be trusted on sensitive machines.

A security tool should never become the risk. AI Sentry is designed so it can run where the stakes are highest.

  • Your data stays on your deviceScans run locally. Reports are saved where you choose, and nothing is sent to us.
  • Read-only by designAI Sentry reports and recommends. It never deletes, quarantines or modifies anything on its own.
  • Works offlineNo internet connection required to scan, suited to restricted and air-gapped environments.
