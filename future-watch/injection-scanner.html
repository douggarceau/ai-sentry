Prompt-Injection Scanner

AI assistants and agents read text people can't see. Paste a web page, email or document, or load a file, to find instructions hidden for an AI before you ask one to work with it.

Runs in your browser. Nothing you enter is sent anywhere.

It checks for invisible Unicode characters (including hidden "tag" text), text hidden with CSS, HTML comments, instructions in alt text and metadata, phrases that try to override an AI's instructions, data-leaking image links and encoded blocks. To view a page's HTML, right-click it and choose View Page Source.
